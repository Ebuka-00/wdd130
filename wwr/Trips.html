Los Dientes del Diablo

Our most intense run, this half-day class IV trip winds through jagged rock formations and powerful standing waves. Recommended for experienced rafters seeking a real adrenaline rush.

Family Float Trip

A relaxed, full-day class I–II trip perfect for first-timers and young children. Enjoy scenic canyon views, a riverside lunch, and gentle rapids the whole family can share.

Sunset Run

A two-hour evening trip combining moderate class II–III rapids with golden-hour views over the canyon. A favorite for couples and small groups looking for something memorable.
