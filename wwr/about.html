Wild Rapids Rafting

At Wild Rapids Rafting, our mission is to deliver safe, thrilling, and unforgettable white water adventures for every guest, guided by experienced professionals who love the river as much as you do.

History

Founded over two decades ago by a small group of river guides, Wild Rapids Rafting began with a single raft and a passion for adventure. Since then, we've grown into one of the region's most trusted rafting outfitters, welcoming thousands of guests each year while staying true to our roots of safety, community, and respect for the river.
